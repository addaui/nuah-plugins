# Contributing

Contributions are welcome: bug reports, fixes, documentation, plugins and ideas.

- For anything larger than a small fix, open an issue first so we can agree on the direction.
- `cargo test --workspace` should pass before you open a pull request.
- Contributions are reviewed in the usual way. A change may be accepted, may need edits, or may be declined.
